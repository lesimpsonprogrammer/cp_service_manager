The Momentum Data Resource Glossary

The Momentum Knowledge Hub — explore connected terminology across HR, Payroll, Finance, and Data to discover how business concepts connect.

Human Resources

HRIS (Human Resources Information System)
The system of record for employee data — personal details, job information, and employment history.
HCM (Human Capital Management)
A broader platform that typically combines the HRIS with talent, performance, time, and often payroll functions.
Employee Record
The complete set of information about one employee, including identifiers, position, compensation, and status.
Onboarding
The process and data steps required to bring a new hire into every system they need on day one.
Benefits Enrollment
The elections an employee makes for health, retirement, and other benefits, and the data feeds that send them to carriers.

Payroll

Gross Pay
Total earnings for a pay period before any taxes or deductions are taken out.
Net Pay
The amount an employee actually receives after taxes and deductions.
Pay Period
The recurring span of time an employee is paid for, such as weekly, biweekly, or semi-monthly.
Payroll Register
A report listing every employee's earnings, taxes, and deductions for a payroll run.
Year-to-Date (YTD) Balances
Running totals of earnings, taxes, and deductions for the year. They must carry over accurately during a payroll conversion.
Payroll Conversion
Moving payroll from one provider or system to another, including employee setup, tax details, and YTD balances.

Finance

ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)
An integrated system that manages core operations such as finance, purchasing, inventory, and vendors.
General Ledger (GL)
The central record of every financial transaction, organized by account.
Chart of Accounts
The structured list of account codes an organization uses to categorize transactions in the general ledger.
Cost Center
A department or unit that costs are tracked against for budgeting and reporting.
Reconciliation
Comparing two sets of records to confirm they agree and to explain any differences.

Data

Legacy System
An older platform an organization still depends on, often the source of data in a migration.
Data Cleansing
Finding and correcting duplicates, errors, missing values, and inconsistent formatting.
Data Mapping
Defining how each field in a source system corresponds to a field in the destination system.
Data Validation
Checking data against business rules to confirm it is complete, accurate, and correctly formatted.
Data Migration
Moving data from one system to another, usually as part of an implementation or upgrade.
ETL (Extract, Transform, Load)
The process of pulling data from a source, cleaning and reshaping it, and loading it into a destination.
Master File
A single, consolidated, authoritative dataset built from several sources.
API
A defined set of rules that lets two systems exchange data. Learn more about APIs.
Data Connector
A configured, reusable pipeline that moves data between systems. Learn more about connectors.
Webhook
An automatic message a system sends when an event happens. Learn more about webhooks.
