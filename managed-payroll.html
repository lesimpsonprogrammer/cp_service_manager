Managed Payroll: What It Is, Why It Matters

What Is Managed Payroll? Managed payroll hands the ongoing mechanics of paying people — calculating wages, withholding and remitting taxes, filing with the right agencies, keeping up with changing state and local requirements — to a team that runs it as its core discipline, rather than as one more task on an internal team's plate.

Payroll is unforgiving of small mistakes: a missed filing deadline or a misapplied tax rate becomes a penalty, a correction cycle, or an employee paid incorrectly. Managed payroll exists to keep those mistakes from happening in the first place.

Our Managed Payroll Services

  • Payroll Processing — Accurate, on-time pay runs handled end to end, from hours and earnings through final disbursement.
  • Quarterly Tax Filings — Federal, state, and local payroll tax filings prepared and submitted on schedule, every quarter.
  • State Unemployment Tax Research — Rate verification and research so unemployment tax is calculated and remitted correctly across every state you operate in.
  • New Payroll Set-up — Clean, correctly configured payroll setup for new entities, new states, or a switch in payroll platform.
