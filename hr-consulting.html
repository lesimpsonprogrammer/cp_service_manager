About Momentum Data Solutions

Momentum Data Solutions helps organizations prepare, connect, and continuously support their people operations before, during, and after major HR system changes — ensuring HR decisions stay compliant, consistent, and defensible at every stage.

HR Consulting: What It Is, Why It Matters

What Is HR Consulting? HR consulting is outside expertise brought in to guide the HR decisions that carry legal, financial, or cultural risk — policy design, compliance interpretation, workforce planning, and the day-to-day judgment calls that don't have a clean answer in an employee handbook.

For organizations without a full internal HR bench, or those navigating a change — a new state's employment law, a reduction in force, a policy overhaul — a consultant supplies experience the situation demands, exactly when it's needed.

Our HR Consulting Services

  • HR Consulting — Hands-on guidance on policy, employee relations, and workforce strategy from professionals who've handled the situation before.
  • Compliance Management — Interpretation and application of federal, state, and local employment law so policies hold up under audit or dispute.
  • HR on Demand — Flexible, as-needed HR support for organizations that need expertise without a full-time hire.
  • SHRM-Certified HR Professionals — Work led by credentialed practitioners held to the field's recognized standard of practice.
